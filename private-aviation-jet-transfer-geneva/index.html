Questions.

Can your chauffeur meet me at the aircraft in Geneva?

Yes. We have tarmac access at Geneva Airport: your chauffeur meets you at the foot of the stairs when the FBO and handling agent allow it, otherwise inside the private aviation terminal.

Which private terminals do you serve in Geneva?

The C3 private aviation terminal, Jet Aviation, TAG Aviation and Signature Flight Support, plus Sion Airport and Zurich Airport.

Do you work 24/7 for private jet arrivals?

Yes. 24/7 operations and flight monitoring, with priority WhatsApp dispatch in under 10 minutes.

Can you send several cars for one arrival?

Yes. Mercedes S-Class and V-Class VIP vans on the same arrival, coordinated by one contact. See our fleet.

Which currencies do you accept?

Swiss francs, euros and US dollars.
