How it works

A car and a private chauffeur at your disposal in Geneva, by the hour. 20 km are included per hour. Your chauffeur waits between stops and follows your day as it changes.

For what

Meetings across Geneva and the international organisations. Roadshows. Dinners and evenings. Events at Palexpo. A day between Geneva, Lausanne and Montreux.

The cars

Mercedes E-Class from CHF 80 per hour. Mercedes V-Class VIP from CHF 100, for up to 7. Mercedes S-Class from CHF 130. Recent cars, kept immaculate.

The chauffeur

Professional and discreet, in French and English. What is said in the car stays in the car.

Beyond Geneva

Lausanne, Montreux, Zurich or a day in the Alps. For companies and delegations, see corporate chauffeur service.

Booking

Send your date and programme. We reply with a fixed price within the hour, 7am to 11pm. One contact, from booking to the last stop.

Questions.

How much is a chauffeur by the hour in Geneva?

From CHF 80 per hour in a Mercedes E-Class, CHF 100 in a V-Class and CHF 130 in an S-Class, with 20 km included per hour.

Does the chauffeur wait during meetings and dinners?

Yes. That is the service: your chauffeur and car stay with you between meetings, outside the dinner, until your day is done.

Can I keep the same chauffeur for several days?

Yes. For roadshows and longer stays, we keep the same chauffeur and car whenever possible.

Can we leave Geneva?

Yes. Lausanne, Montreux, Zurich or the Alps. Your price is fixed and confirmed before your day starts.

How do I book?

Send your date and programme with the form or on WhatsApp. We reply within the hour, from 7am to 11pm.
